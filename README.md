Oxford ENT Head & Neck Surgery – Homepage Redesign
1. Website Overview
Oxford ENT Head & Neck Surgery is the private consultant practice of Mr Priy Silva, a Consultant ENT and Head & Neck Surgeon based in Oxford, United Kingdom. The practice provides specialist consultations, diagnostic assessments and surgical care for adults and children with ear, nose, throat and head and neck conditions.

The redesigned homepage focuses on presenting the practice as an experienced, specialist-led medical service. It prioritises clinical credibility, clear service information, patient confidence and straightforward access to consultation and clinic contact details.
2. Main Improvements
1. Clearer Specialist Positioning and Professional Credibility
The homepage gives greater prominence to Mr Priy Silva's consultant expertise, qualifications, surgical experience and specialist clinical leadership. This creates a clearer understanding of the practice's specialist focus and establishes professional credibility from the first interaction.
2. Improved Service Organisation and Information Hierarchy
ENT consultations, ear and hearing conditions, nose and sinus problems, throat disorders, and complex head and neck surgical services are organised into clearer content sections. This makes the range of available treatments easier to understand and helps visitors identify relevant specialist services.
3. More Accessible Contact and Clinic Information
Consultation and contact information is presented more clearly, including the two Oxford clinic locations at Nuffield Health Oxford, The Manor Hospital, and GenesisCare Oxford. Telephone and email contact details are easier to identify, supporting a more direct enquiry journey.
3. Design Improvements
- Visual Presentation: A refined medical design establishes a professional and specialist healthcare identity.
- Layout: Clear section organisation improves content hierarchy and makes important information easier to navigate.
- Typography: Consistent typography improves readability and creates a more structured presentation.
- User Experience: Clearer service descriptions and more accessible contact information support patient navigation.
- Content Consistency: Consultant credentials, clinical expertise, treatment information and clinic details follow a more consistent presentation.
- Overall Appearance: The redesigned homepage presents a more cohesive and professional digital presence aligned with the practice's specialist medical services.

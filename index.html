Meet your consultant

Experience grounded in specialist training and research.

Mr Priy Silva is a Consultant ENT and Head & Neck Surgeon with an NHS practice at Oxford University Hospitals NHS Foundation Trust and private practice in Oxford.

After graduating from medical school in 1999, he completed higher surgical training in Manchester and postgraduate research in head and neck cancer at the Christie Cancer Centre, where he was awarded an MD. He subsequently undertook advanced surgical fellowships in Oxford and Australia.

His professional work includes clinical research, medical education and specialist head and neck practice. His published biography reports more than 30 peer-reviewed medical articles.
